## What this does and does not establish

**Does establish:** for every feature these six checks exercise (single-event TVC survival curves, competing-risks CIF, permutation/landmark importance oracles, `PEScore` convergence, and cross-implementation agreement on a static PH DGP), the fitted model's predictions move toward a known closed-form truth as expected, or — where a check is manual-only — a specific, named CSV record exists from a specific past run at a stated R.

**Does not establish:** that every listed check currently *passing in CI* is sufficient to catch every possible statistical regression. Concretely out of scope:
- **Manual-only gates** (rows #2-#4's R=30/R=50 statistical decisions) are not re-run automatically; a regression that only shows up at that replication count, and not in the `slow`-tier R=10 pilots, would not be caught by `pytest -m slow` alone.
- **No n-sweep / asymptotic study** beyond Slice 1's two-point (200, 5000) check — general convergence rate claims are not established.
- **No landmark-estimator or non-TVC-static-DGP cross-check against an external tool** — Slice 2's external-tool comparison is TVC-free by construction (`CoxPHFitter` can't handle TVC); no equivalent check exists for the TVC case against, e.g., `CoxTimeVaryingFitter` on a *known-truth* (as opposed to real-data) DGP.
- **No competing-risks cross-check against an external reference tool** on a known-truth DGP (only against `randomForestSRC` via manual R scripts, per `docs/plans/research.md` Q5 — not part of any automated gate).
- **No calibration/coverage study** (see `docs/plans/design.md` Part B) — these checks validate point-estimate convergence and relative agreement, not interval coverage.
