Generated by `bench/criteria/` (`python -m bench.criteria.run`, then `python -m bench.criteria.summarise`) at commit `a49cf55`.

and the configuration used is in `config.json`. Full run: 395 s on 10 cores (2026-09-26).

## Recommendation

1. **Keep `split_criterion="logrank"` as the default.** No challenger passed the pre-registered rule:
   - `grouped_lik` and `poisson` are indistinguishable from log-rank on landmark IBS and calibration;
   - both are slightly (not significantly) worse on the known-truth simulation;
   - `km_gini` is clearly worse on the panel.
2. **Keep `aggregate="hazard"` (D11 unchanged).** `survival` averaging did not improve calibration on both landmark datasets. It was slightly worse on the known-truth simulation.
3. **The losing criteria are removed (plan item 9).** They stay in git history (`65a0213`, `be4a90d`, harness `c4aeebc`). `Profile.exposure` / `times` / `n_units` and `SplitCriterion::score(…, n_units_right)` stay as the extension point for future criteria (user decision; about 3% fit time). `landmark_cross_validate(return_predictions=True)` stays.

## Arms and protocol (summary)

- **Arms:** 4 criteria (`logrank`, `grouped_lik`, `poisson`, `km_gini`) × `aggregate` ∈ {`hazard`, `survival`}. `km_gini` runs only on landmark data, with τ = w.
- **Common settings:**
  - 300 trees, seed 0 for every arm;
  - each arm tuned separately over `min_ids_leaf` ∈ {5, 15, 50} × `max_features` ∈ {sqrt, None}.
- **Protocol A (decision datasets):** nested `landmark_cross_validate`, tuned on inner IBS.
  - **PBC2:** 290 patients, landmarks 1–4 y, w = 2 y, `GroupKFold(5)` outer / `(3)` inner. 1,038 scored landmark rows.
  - **Panel** (`tests/sim_panel.py`): 2,000 units × 96 periods, landmarks every 3 periods, w = 6, `RollingOriginSplit(5 / 3, test_size=6, gap=6)`. 3,960 scored rows from 596 units.
  - **Metrics:** pooled out-of-fold IBS and Brier(w); ICI and calibration slope from 10 risk deciles.
  - **Uncertainty:** paired subject-cluster bootstrap, B = 500, percentile 95% intervals.
- **Protocol B:**
  - **`tests/sim.py`:** 20 independent replications, tuned by inner `GroupKFold(3)` concordance. Metric: ISE of path predictions against the true S; paired t-intervals.
  - **Cunningham & Lemke war data:** new-war `GroupKFold(5)`, counting-process C. Supporting only.

## Decision rule (as pre-registered)

Every check is in `decisions.csv`. A challenger needed (i)–(iv) at both aggregations.

| decision | outcome | failed checks |
|---|---|---|
| default → `grouped_lik` | **not adopted** | (i) sim ISE worse, not better; (ii) no dataset where its IBS interval excludes 0 in its favour |
| default → `poisson` | **not adopted** | (i) sim ISE worse, not better; (ii) same as above |
| default → `km_gini` | **not adopted** | (i) no sim arm (ineligible by design); (ii) panel IBS interval favours log-rank |
| D11: `aggregate` → `survival` | **not adopted** | ICI interval includes 0 on PBC2 (−0.010 [−0.018, +0.014]) and favours `hazard` in point estimate on the panel (+0.003 [−0.003, +0.005]) |

Fit time (iv) passed for every arm on the landmark data: `grouped_lik` is 1.5–1.9× log-rank. On `sim.py` it is about 6×, which the rule did not check. `grouped_lik` evaluates logarithms for every event time of every candidate, while log-rank uses precomputed multiply-adds.

## Observations (descriptive, not part of the rule)

- **The criteria barely matter here.** On both landmark datasets, `grouped_lik`, `poisson` and log-rank agree to about 0.0005 IBS. Tuning chose similar settings for every arm: `min_ids_leaf=5` on PBC2 (every arm and fold) and 50 on the panel (all but three arm-folds, and `km_gini` mostly 5); `max_features` varied. The split rule is not the bottleneck on these data.
- **`km_gini` fails on landmark stacks.** On the panel its forest predicts almost the same risk for everyone: the risk SD is 0.001, vs 0.10 for log-rank, so the slope of 25 is meaningless. The likely cause is its unit convention. In a landmark stack each subject has a row at many landmarks, so most splits put a subject on both sides and count it in both children. The Gini gain is then negative, and the tree rarely splits. On PBC2 (4 landmarks) it splits and is only mildly worse. A row-weighted impurity or an IPCW squared-loss criterion would avoid this; both are out of scope (plan item 11).
- **Aggregation:**
  - On PBC2, `survival` averaging calibrated better in point estimate for every criterion (ICI 0.023–0.028 vs 0.032–0.035, with slopes closer to 1). The interval still includes 0.
  - On the panel and on `sim.py`, `hazard` was as good or better.
  - The D11 argument (conditional survival stays consistent across origins) holds. Revisit only if a larger new-subject dataset repeats the PBC2 pattern.
- **War data:** log-rank has the best C. `grouped_lik` is lowest and has the most fold-to-fold variation.

## Limitations

- Four datasets, one seed per arm and one outer split per dataset: small effects (< 0.001 IBS) cannot be resolved.
- The cluster bootstrap keeps the censoring weights of the original fit, and it resamples the outer CV predictions without refitting, so it ignores tuning variability.
- ICI and slope come from 10 deciles pooled over folds and landmarks. The slope is unstable when predictions barely vary (see `km_gini`).
- The selected `min_ids_leaf` sits at the edge of the grid on both landmark datasets (5 on PBC2, 50 on the panel). Every arm was tuned on the same grid, so the comparison stays fair, but the absolute scores may improve with a wider grid.
- `km_gini` could not pass check (i) by construction. That is a consequence of the pre-registered rule, not evidence against it on `sim.py`.
